ComfyUI-H3-ExactMasterMemory v0.10.0 - final touches

H3RDLoraStack
- Removed: Facial Realism CloseUp and Cinematic Realism. Four named LoRAs remain (Realism People,
  Motion Continuity Repair, Spatial Physics, LMS Sharpness) plus the two custom slots.

H3RDHQGate (upscale switch)
- New field "upscaler_model" (default: auto-detect) and new output "model_name". The switch finds the
  latent upscaler file under its current or its older official name and hands it to the upscaler node,
  so the workflow no longer depends on which of the two names is on disk.

H3RDLoadVideo
- New browser script web/h3rd_load_video.js for the preview ComfyUI shows on the node: it loops without
  sound, plays sound while the mouse is over it, and plays only the frames that will be loaded
  (skip_first_frames, skip_last_frames, frame_load_cap). The node works without the script.
